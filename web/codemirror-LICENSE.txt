CodeMirror 6, bundled into web/codemirror.js (ADR-0034).

Vendored rather than loaded from a CDN: a CDN is a third party who can see every
visitor, and this page's claim is that there is not one. Rebuild it with the recipe
in web/README.md. Exact versions in this bundle:

  @codemirror/state@6.5.2
  @codemirror/view@6.36.4
  @codemirror/commands@6.8.0
  @codemirror/language@6.10.8
  @lezer/highlight@1.2.1
  @lezer/common@1.5.2
  @lezer/lr@1.4.10
  style-mod@4.1.3
  w3c-keyname@2.2.8
